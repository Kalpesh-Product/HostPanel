# Basic Plan Overview

The **Basic** plan is the default plan every new WONO workspace starts on. It gives a host
the essentials to run a business on the platform: a public website, Nomad listings, visitor
management, customer support, organization and access management, and basic analytics.

![Basic plan Dashboard with sidebar, greeting, overview, and quick actions](/img/basic-plan/dashboard-overview.png)

:::note
The screenshots in this guide are taken from a sample Basic-plan workspace. Names, counts,
available actions, and notices may differ by account, plan configuration, and permissions.
Seeing a module or section label does not guarantee every action inside it is available to you.
:::

## What Basic includes

By default, Basic unlocks:

- **Dashboard** — your workspace home page.
- **Customer Support** — raise and track issues with the WONO team.
- **Website Builder** — create and publish your website, and manage its Leads, Reviews, and Careers pages.
- **Nomad Listings** — publish business spaces on the WONO Nomads network and manage their listings, leads, reviews, and verification.
- **All Leads** — review website and Nomad enquiries together.
- **Visitor Management** — record and review front-desk visitors (standard visitor flow).
- **Profile** — manage your personal profile, company profile, plan and billing, and password.
- **Organization Management** and **Access Grants** — invite users and control module access.
- **Analytics** — review activity across the modules enabled for your unit.

Some areas are visible but locked on Basic, such as visitor bookings, walk-in bookings, unit
tours, and cross-module report export. These become available on higher plans or through
additional grants.

## How the navigation is organized

The Host Panel sidebar groups modules into four areas:

- **Common Modules** — baseline modules available to every workspace.
- **Key Apps** — Website Builder, Nomad Listings, All Leads, and Visitor Management.
- **Core Modules** — Organization Management, Access Grants, and Analytics.
- **Add-ons** — the expanded set, including Profile and other modules.

The active plan is shown at the top of the sidebar as **PLAN - BASIC**, and the **Search modules**
field helps you jump to a module by name.
