# Company Profile

Open **Add Ons > Profile > Company Profile**. This page presents the active workspace identity and
business-location information used throughout the Host Panel.

![Company Profile page](/img/basic-plan/company-profile.png)

## Company identity

The header shows the company name, logo controls, and current plan label. The company logo can be
reviewed and, where permitted, replaced.

:::note
Confirm branding with an authorized company administrator before making a change, because logo
updates affect the workspace identity.
:::

## Business location details

The page displays the unit name, company name, brand name, address, country, state, city, type of
vertical, selected plan, timezone, and currency. These values originate from the business-location
onboarding flow. Review them carefully, because they can affect displayed identity, scheduling,
reporting, and monetary values elsewhere in the application.

## Plan action

An **Upgrade Plan** action is available from the company profile. It starts the plan-selection flow
and is not required for normal Basic-plan use. Review plan scope and pricing before submitting an
upgrade request.

![Upgrade Plan action from Company Profile](/img/basic-plan/upgrade-plan.png)

## Danger zone

The **Danger Zone** contains **Delete My Account**.

:::danger
Account deletion is a destructive administrative action. It requires explicit confirmation, clear
data-retention information, and an authorized decision maker.
:::

## Recommended review workflow

1. Confirm the company and brand identity.
2. Compare the unit name and address with the approved business location.
3. Verify country, state, city, timezone, and currency.
4. Confirm that the Basic plan label is expected.
5. Use upgrade or deletion actions only after internal approval.
