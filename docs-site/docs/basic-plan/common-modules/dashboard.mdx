# Dashboard

The Dashboard is the first workspace page a founder or super administrator sees after signing
in. It brings together the Basic plan status, key activity numbers, shortcuts, and first-use
guidance.

![Basic plan Dashboard with sidebar, greeting, overview, and quick actions](/img/basic-plan/dashboard-overview.png)

## Open the Dashboard

Select **Dashboard** under **Common Modules** in the left navigation. The breadcrumb reads
`Common Modules > Dashboard`.

The sidebar identifies the active plan as **PLAN - BASIC** and groups navigation into Common
Modules, Key Apps, Core Modules, and Add-ons. The **Search modules** field helps you locate a
module by name.

## Header and workspace context

The **Dashboard** heading is followed by a **Guide** control and the **Basic Plan** badge. Below
it, a greeting identifies the signed-in user, and the date, live workspace-local time, and
location show the context in which the Dashboard is being viewed. The top bar also provides a
profile area and a notification control.

![Dashboard heading, guide, plan badge, greeting, date, and location](/img/basic-plan/dashboard-header.png)

If your account belongs to more than one workspace, a workspace switcher may appear. Switching
workspaces changes the working context for every module.

## Use the page guide

Select **Guide** beside the Dashboard heading to open a walkthrough of the page. It covers
navigation, the breadcrumb, workspace switching, notifications, the plan strip, the overview,
and quick actions.

Because some targets only appear when a workspace has activity, or when you have the relevant
access, the number of steps actually shown can vary. You can reopen the Guide at any time from
the same control.

## Plan status and upgrade options

The blue plan strip confirms that the workspace is on **Basic** and highlights Professional
features such as meeting room bookings, ticketing, and Sales modules.

![Upgrade options opened from the Basic plan strip](/img/basic-plan/upgrade-options.gif)

Select the strip or **Upgrade** to review the available plans. The window offers **Professional**
and **Customise** options. Selecting a plan sends a request for review — it does not by itself
confirm that the plan changed or that payment succeeded. Close the window without selecting a
plan to remain on Basic.

:::note
The Dashboard may also show listing, verification, or trial-related notices. These are
conditional and do not appear for every Basic-plan account.
:::

## Understand the Overview cards

The Overview area can show three cards when their corresponding modules are enabled and granted
to you:

![Basic plan Overview cards](/img/basic-plan/overview-cards.gif)

- **Visitors Today** — visitor records dated today in the current workspace view. The smaller
  number reports visitors with a `checked_in` status in the loaded list. Select the card to open
  [Visitor Management](/docs/basic-plan/key-apps/visitor-management/overview).
- **Website Leads** — the total leads returned for the current company or workspace. The smaller
  number counts leads in **Pending** status awaiting follow-up. Select it to open
  [Website Leads](/docs/basic-plan/key-apps/website-builder/website-leads).
- **Active Members** — active members, with the total member count beneath. Select it to open
  [Organization Management](/docs/basic-plan/core-modules/organization-management).

:::note
The numbers shown are example data, not subscription limits. Dashboard visitor calculations use
a list request limited to 100 records, so a busy workspace can have incomplete visitor counts.
The smaller "currently on-site" number is derived from status across the loaded list, not only
visits created today.
:::

## Use Quick Actions

Quick Actions are direct links to modules. They are built from the workspace module catalogue
and your access grants, so the exact cards and their order can differ between accounts.

![Example Quick Actions available to one Basic plan workspace](/img/basic-plan/quick-actions.png)

Selecting a card opens its module; the module's own guide covers the next workflow.

## Getting Started for a new workspace

When the loaded lead and visitor lists are both empty, the lower part of the page shows
**Getting Started** with three suggested actions:

![Getting Started cards for a workspace without leads or visitors](/img/basic-plan/getting-started.gif)

1. **Build and publish your website** — opens [Website Builder](/docs/basic-plan/key-apps/website-builder/overview) to begin site setup.
2. **Share your website link** — opens [Website Leads](/docs/basic-plan/key-apps/website-builder/website-leads), where enquiries from the published site can be followed up. The card is guidance; it does not itself share a link.
3. **Log your first visitor** — opens [Visitor Management](/docs/basic-plan/key-apps/visitor-management/overview) to record a visitor.

These cards are navigation shortcuts, not a completion tracker. The section disappears as soon
as either loaded list contains activity, even if you have not completed all three suggested
actions.

## Dashboard after activity begins

When leads or visitor records are available, the lower area shows activity panels instead of
Getting Started (subject to module access):

- **Recent Leads** — up to five recent enquiries.
- **Lead Status** — groups leads by Pending, Contacted, Closed, and Rejected.
- **Recent Visitors** — up to five recent visitor records with status.
- **Monthly Visitor Trend (FY)** — month labels from April through March.

**View all** opens the corresponding full module.

:::note
The visitor trend is calculated from the same limited visitor list and groups records by
calendar month. Treat it as a quick indicator, not a complete financial-year report.
:::

## Ask WoNo help

The floating button at the lower-right opens the **Ask WoNo** panel, which shows suggested
topics such as getting started, login or password help, and plan or billing questions.

![Ask WoNo panel opened from the Dashboard](/img/basic-plan/ask-wono-panel.png)
