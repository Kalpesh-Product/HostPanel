# Standard Visitor

**Standard Visitor** records a normal front-desk visitor and checks that person into the location.
Open **Key Apps > Visitor Management > New Frontdesk Action**, then select **Standard Visitor**.

:::note
Under Basic permissions, **Unit Tour**, **Walk-in Booking**, **Verify Booking**,
**Department Visitor**, and **Tenant Company Visitor** are locked.
:::

![Frontdesk Action page with Standard Visitor and locked advanced modes](/img/basic-plan/frontdesk-action.png)

## Choose new or existing visitor

Use **New Visitor** when the person has not been recorded before. Use **Existing Visitor** to
search previous visitor records and reuse the correct person's information. Always confirm identity
before reusing an existing record.

## Enter visitor information

For a new visitor, complete first name, last name, gender, phone number, email address, country,
state, and city. Choose **Individual** or **Company** as the visitor type. When **Company** is
selected, enter the visitor's company name.

![Standard Visitor information and visit details form](/img/basic-plan/standard-visitor-info.gif)

## Enter visit details

Keep **Standard Visitor** selected. Choose the purpose and enter a useful visit note. Purposes can
include **Exploring Services**, **General Visit**, **Unit Enquiry**, and **Delivery** — available
values can change with configuration.

![Standard Visitor visit type and purpose fields](/img/basic-plan/standard-visitor-purpose.gif)

## Check in and print the pass

Review required fields and select **Check In Visitor**. After success, the system generates a
visitor pass containing details such as name, badge number, purpose, check-in time, phone number,
and host or destination. Use **Print Badge** if required by the front-desk policy. The visitor then
appears in [Daily Visitors](/docs/basic-plan/key-apps/visitor-management/daily-visitors).

## Correct use and privacy

- Collect only information required for the visit.
- Confirm phone and email before submission.
- Do not use another person's existing visitor record.
- Keep visitor data and printed badges private.
- Check the visitor out when the visit ends.
