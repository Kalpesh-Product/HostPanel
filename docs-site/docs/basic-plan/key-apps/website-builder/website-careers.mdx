# Website Careers

**Careers** lets you publish job openings on the business website and manage applications
submitted by candidates. Open **Key Apps > Website Builder > Careers**.

:::note
Careers remains locked until the business creates its first website. If selected before that
point, the page displays **Please create your website first**.
:::

## Careers page layout

The page contains two main tabs:

- **Job Openings** — creating and managing vacancies;
- **Applications** — reviewing candidates who apply through the website.

**Job Openings** summary cards show **Total Jobs**, **Active**, **Total Vacancies**, and
**Filled**. Status filters and search help you locate an opening, and **Publish Job** starts
creation of a vacancy.

![Careers page with Job Openings, Applications counts and Publish Job](/img/basic-plan/website-careers.gif)

## Publish a job opening

Select **Publish Job** and complete the required vacancy information. The form can include:

- job title and department;
- number of vacancies;
- employment type and location;
- job description;
- responsibilities;
- requirements.

Review the wording, vacancy count, employment type, and location before publishing. Confirm that
the website Careers page is enabled and that the published job appears correctly on the public
site.

## Manage job openings

Use the **Active** and **Inactive** filters to separate currently advertised roles from closed or
disabled openings. Search by the supported title or department fields. When a role is no longer
accepting applications, update its status so candidates do not apply to an unavailable vacancy.

:::note
The page may provide bulk upload using the recruitment job template, which keeps one job opening
per row and requires fields such as title, department, employment type, vacancy total, status,
and active state.
:::

## Review applications

Open **Applications** to view candidates who submitted the website application form. The public
form can collect name, email, mobile number, location, CV, and additional configured fields.

Applications can progress through stages such as:

- **Screening** — initial review;
- **Interview Scheduled** — after an interview is arranged;
- **Interviewed** — after the discussion is completed;
- **Selected** — the successful candidate.

Use search and filters to locate a candidate, open the application, review the submitted details
and CV, then update the stage only after the corresponding recruitment action has occurred.

## Recommended operating practice

- Publish only approved and complete job descriptions.
- Keep the active vacancy count accurate.
- Disable or close filled positions promptly.
- Limit candidate-data access to authorized users.
- Treat CVs and contact details as sensitive personal information.
- Verify the public Careers page after every important change.
