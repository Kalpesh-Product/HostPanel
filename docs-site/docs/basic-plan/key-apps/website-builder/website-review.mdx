# Website Review

**Website Review** contains customer reviews submitted through the business website and lets you
control their moderation status. Open **Key Apps > Website Builder > Website Review**.

This page is separate from reviews attached to WONO Nomad listings.

## Page summary

The top cards show:

- **Total Reviews** — for all website reviews;
- **Pending** — for reviews waiting for a decision;
- **Approved** — for accepted reviews;
- **Rejected** — for reviews that should not be displayed.

![Website Review page with moderation counts, filters and empty state](/img/basic-plan/website-review.gif)

Use **All**, **Pending**, **Approved**, and **Rejected** to filter the list. Search helps you
locate a review using the fields supported by the page. Approved reviews can be shown publicly,
while inappropriate reviews can be rejected.

## Receive and inspect a review

A review appears after a visitor submits the website review form. When testing a new website:

1. Open the public website and submit a clearly labelled test review.
2. Return to **Website Review** and confirm that **Total** and **Pending** increase.
3. Open the review and verify the reviewer, rating, description, source, and current status.
4. Confirm that the review belongs to the correct company and website.

## Approve or reject

Read the complete review before choosing a status. Approve feedback that meets your business
moderation policy and can be displayed publicly. Reject spam, abusive content, irrelevant
material, duplicate submissions, or content that violates the approved policy.

:::note
Do not reject a review only because it is critical — use a consistent moderation standard.
:::

After the decision, confirm that the review moves to the correct status filter and that the
summary counts update. If the page provides a separate public-display control for approved
reviews, verify its state on the public website as well.

## Search and filtering

Use **Pending** for the daily moderation queue, and **Approved** or **Rejected** when reviewing
past decisions. Clear filters before concluding that a review is missing.

## Recommended moderation practice

- Review **Pending** items regularly.
- Read the full text rather than relying on a list preview.
- Apply one documented moderation policy consistently.
- Verify that approved content is appropriate for public display.
- Keep rejected submissions available for audit where the product permits it.
