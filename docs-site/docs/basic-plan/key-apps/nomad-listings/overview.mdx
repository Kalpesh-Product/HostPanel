# Nomad Listings Overview

**Nomad Listings** is the entry page for publishing business spaces on the WONO Nomads network
and managing the activity created by those listings. Open **Key Apps > Nomad Listings**.

The page routes to four destinations, each with its own documentation:

- **Listings** — your inventory of Nomad listings.
- **Reviews** — feedback submitted for Nomad listings.
- **Leads** — enquiries submitted from Nomad listings.
- **Verify Business** — the blue verified badge for active public listings.

![Nomad Listings hub with Listings, Reviews and Leads](/img/basic-plan/nomad-listings-hub.gif)

## Listings

**Listings** opens the inventory of Nomad listings for the active company. The page displays
capacity, activation state, public visibility, product type, location, and listing actions. It
also provides **Add Listing** and the existing `wono.co` listing claim flow.

→ See [Listings](/docs/basic-plan/key-apps/nomad-listings/listings),
[Add Listing](/docs/basic-plan/key-apps/nomad-listings/add-listing), and
[Edit Listing](/docs/basic-plan/key-apps/nomad-listings/edit-listing).

## Reviews

**Reviews** opens feedback submitted for Nomad listings. You can search and filter by status,
rating, product type, and listing, then approve or reject reviews and control approved public
display where available.

→ See [Nomad Reviews](/docs/basic-plan/key-apps/nomad-listings/reviews).

## Leads

**Leads** opens enquiries submitted from Nomad listings. You can review **Pending** and
**Closed** enquiries. Nomad Leads is source-specific; [All Leads](/docs/basic-plan/key-apps/all-leads)
combines Nomad and website enquiries.

→ See [Nomad Leads](/docs/basic-plan/key-apps/nomad-listings/leads).

## Verify Business

**Verify Business** manages the blue verified badge for active public Nomad listings. It stays
locked until the company has an active public listing or an approved claim for existing `wono.co`
listings. The page includes **Listings**, **Status**, and **Payment History** tabs, and may
include a free verification period followed by paid renewal options.

→ See [Verify Business](/docs/basic-plan/key-apps/nomad-listings/verify-business).

## Recommended first action

For a new account, open **Listings** and add the first business listing. Complete its details,
wait for any required approval or activation, and make it public. Reviews, leads, and verification
become useful after listings are live and visitors begin interacting with them.
