# Verify Business

**Verify Business** manages the blue verified badge for active public WONO Nomad listings. Open
**Key Apps > Nomad Listings > Verify Business**.

:::note
The card and verification action remain unavailable until the company has an active public listing
or an approved claim for existing `wono.co` listings.
:::

![Verify Business page](/img/basic-plan/verify-business.png)

## Listings tab

The **Listings** tab shows company name, listing title, product type, country, state, city,
Master Status, Visibility, and actions. A listing must be active and public before it is eligible
for verification. When available, the public-link action opens the live `wono.co` listing.

The badge action changes according to state: submit for verification, open **Pending Review**, pay
after approval, show or hide an active badge, or renew/change the plan. Confirm the listing and
company before selecting any action.

## Submit verification

Select **Verify Business** and complete the form. Review any prefilled company and onboarding
information, attach the required proof documents, choose the available verification tier when
requested, and submit. The **Status** tab then shows **Pending Review**.

:::note
Do not upload unrelated personal data. Confirm document type, ownership, validity, and
readability before submission.
:::

## Status tab

The **Status** tab shows states such as **Not Verified**, **Under Review**, **Rejected**,
**Approved - Payment Pending**, **Verified**, or **Expired**. It also shows submission dates,
submitted documents, verification period, and follow-up actions.

If rejected, review the reason, correct the information, and use **Edit and Resubmit**. An approved
request can begin with the product's configured free verification period.

## Payment and renewal

When payment is required, select the available plan and continue to the external payment link. A
successful payment activates or extends the verification period. Renewal or plan change begins
after the current active period, so remaining paid days are preserved; if the previous period has
expired, the new period starts immediately.

## Payment History tab

**Payment History** lists paid entries with date, change type, plan, amount, and invoice link. Use
this history to verify completed renewals and retrieve available invoices.

:::note
Do not treat an initiated payment as successful until the payment result and history confirm it.
:::

## Badge visibility

An active verified listing can show or hide the badge without purchasing again. Hiding affects
public badge display; it does not necessarily cancel the paid verification period. Re-enable the
badge from the listing action when required.
