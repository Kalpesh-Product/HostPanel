# Welcome to HostPanel

HostPanel is the admin panel that every Wono host uses to run their space.

If you're a coworking, co-living, or café operator running on Wono, this is your single place to handle the day-to-day: your team, your tenants, bookings, billing, and your public website — all from one dashboard.

You only ever see the tools your plan includes, so the panel stays simple and focused on what your workspace actually uses.

## What you can do with HostPanel

- **Run your space** — set up your organization, workspaces, teams, and departments.
- **Welcome guests** — manage visitors, sign-ins, and access for your space.
- **Handle bookings** — meeting rooms, shared resources, and calendars.
- **Grow your business** — track leads, manage your sales pipeline, and publish your own website.
- **Stay on top of money** — invoicing, plans, and billing.
- **Get help fast** — raise and track support tickets in one place.

## Your plan decides what's available

HostPanel comes in three plans. As your business grows, you can move up to a plan that unlocks more.

### Basic

The starting plan for every new workspace. It covers the essentials you need to get up and running.

### Professional

Everything in Basic, plus the full set of operational tools — tickets, meeting rooms, calendar, sales CRM, and more.

### Custom

Everything in Professional, plus any extra features added specifically for your workspace.

:::tip
You can check which plan you're on at any time under **Profile → Plan & Billing**.
:::

:::note
Some features are switched on per department, so what you see in your panel may look slightly different depending on your role and what your workspace has enabled.
:::
