# Quick Tour

A short look at how HostPanel is laid out, so you know where everything lives.

## The Dashboard

When you sign in, you land on your **Dashboard** — a snapshot of what's happening in your workspace, with quick access to the tools you use most.

## The main navigation

Your sidebar is the main way to get around. What appears there depends on your plan and your role, but it's grouped into a few familiar areas:

- **Dashboard** — your starting point and day-to-day overview.
- **Key Apps** — the everyday working tools: Website Builder, Nomad Listings, All Leads, and Visitor Management.
- **Core Modules** — Organization Management, Access Grants, and Analytics.
- **Common Modules** — shared tools such as Customer Support and other baseline modules.
- **Add-ons** — extra tools that can be enabled for your workspace.
- **Profile** — your personal settings, company profile, plan and billing, and password.

## Your account area

Under **Profile** you can look after your own account:

- **My Profile** — your personal details.
- **Company Profile** — your organization's information.
- **Plan & Billing** — see your current plan and manage billing.
- **Change Password** — keep your account secure.

:::tip
Only seeing the tools you actually use? That's expected — modules you don't need are hidden so your panel stays uncluttered.
:::
